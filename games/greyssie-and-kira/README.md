# Where Are Greyssie and Kira?

Our two grey cats are hiding somewhere in a three-storey dollhouse. Shake the
treats to hear where they are, lure the shy one out with the laser dot, pick
them both up and bring them to their dinner bowls. One game is one day:
morning, afternoon, evening and a warm, cosy night, then dinner, a cuddle on
the sofa and bedtime.

| Seat | Who | Does | Pad | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Walker (shoes, blue) | the grown-up | walks, climbs the stairs, opens and closes hiding spots, picks up a cat (one on each shoulder), puts them down at the bowls | stick / d-pad; up/down on the stairs; A opens / picks up / puts down; Start pauses; B (in the pause) goes back | WASD; Shift; Esc pauses; Q back |
| 2 Laser (pointer, yellow) | a 5-year-old | moves the red dot in 4 directions; shy or high-up cats stalk it and pounce | d-pad / stick; A wiggles the dot | arrow keys; Enter wiggles |
| 3 Treats (bag, green) | a 2-year-old | shakes the treat bag: the nearest cat meows back, a bubble pops in its room and the little house map lights it up | any button | Space or any other key |

- **Walker alone:** hold A to shake the treats; the dot trots ahead of you.
- **No toddler:** the bag shakes itself every 12 seconds.
- **No laser:** a shy cat comes out on its own after 3 seconds.
- Nothing can go wrong: an empty spot always hides something funny (a toy
  mouse, a sock, a ball of wool, a dust bunny), a hidden cat's tail pokes out
  further after a minute, and after 90 seconds it walks out and meows at you.

Seats fill in the order devices press a button; pads and keyboard mix, and
one keyboard is enough for all three.

## Run it

```
make setup    # once: shared Godot + GUT, Python deps via uv
make start    # play
make demo     # watch it play itself (SHOT_SCREEN=join plays a whole day)
make help     # tests, assets, frame captures, export, tmux
```

To make it our real house and cats, see [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) "Making it our
real house and cats".
