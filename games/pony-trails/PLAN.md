# Pony Trails

Groom your horses in the stable, ride the countryside trail with a foal
trotting beside the mare, and finish at the horse show where everyone wins a
rosette.

Status: plan (2026-09-27)

## Why it fits our family

The kids love horses, so the game gives them the whole horse day, not just
riding:
- caring for them (brushing, carrots, hooves, braiding the mane);
- riding out;
- showing off.

The 5-year-old gets **their own horse on screen**, a foal tied to the mare
by an invisible tether, so it can never get lost or left behind.

- **The 5-year-old:**
  - In the stable they brush the mud off with the brush in 4 directions.
  - On the trail they steer the foal between three lanes to grab apples,
    flowers and lucky horseshoes.
  - At the show they ride the foal's mini course.
- **The 2-year-old:** ANY button is a big happy **NEIGH**. Both horses
  whinny and toss their manes, and nearby animals answer:
  - rabbits pop up, sheep baa, birds fly;
  - the neigh opens the field gates;
  - it gives the mare a carrot in the stable;
  - it rings the start bell at the show.

  It is the most satisfying button in the game and it can never hurt the
  run.
- **The adult:**
  - In the stable: the hooves and the saddle.
  - On the trail: riding the mare (walk, trot, canter, and jumps with an
    assist).
  - At the show: riding the jumping course.
- **Real horse things, gently taught without text:** horses eat hay and
  carrots, need water and brushing, and wear horseshoes; foals stay close to
  their mothers; coats have names (bay, chestnut, palomino, grey, pinto).

## Roles and controls

| Seat | Role | Pad | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rider** (adult): stable = hoof pick and saddle; trail = rides the mare (speed + jump); show = rides the course | left stick / d-pad, A = jump / use tool, Start pauses, B = back (pause only) | kb:0: WASD, Shift = jump / tool, Esc pauses, Q back |
| 2 | **Foal and brush** (5-year-old): stable = moves the brush and scrubs; trail = moves the foal up/down between 3 lanes, A = hop (grab); show = foal's mini jumps | d-pad / stick 4-way, A = scrub / hop | kb:1: arrows, Enter = scrub / hop |
| 3 | **Neigh** (2-year-old): whinny, carrot, gate, bell | any button | kb:2: Space or any other key |

With fewer seats:
- **1 seat:** the foal follows in the lane of the nearest apple and hops by
  itself; the rider's B neighs.
- **No 5-year-old:** the brush scrubs by itself and slowly, and the foal
  follows automatically.
- **No toddler:** gates open when you arrive, and the mare neighs on her
  own at the fun moments.

## The day: three stages (about 8–9 minutes)

The select screen can start from any stage (a horseshoe, a tree and a
rosette icon).

### Stage 1: the stable (about 2 minutes)

The mare stands big in her stall, at 2x, side view, with the foal peeking
over the half-door. She is muddy after a night in the paddock: 4 mud patches
on her head, back, legs and tail.

- **Brush (5-year-old):** move the brush with the 4 directions; holding A
  scrubs, with bristle sparkles. A patch fades in about 2 s of scrubbing. A
  generous hitbox: the brush snaps to the nearest patch in the pressed
  direction.
- **Carrot and pet (2-year-old):** any press offers a carrot. The mare
  munches (with an ear wiggle), the foal pokes its head in and "steals"
  every third carrot, and there is a neigh plus hearts.
- **Hooves and saddle (adult):** a hand cursor. Point at each hoof and
  press A: she lifts the hoof, a pebble pops out, and a shiny horseshoe
  glints. When all 4 are done, the saddle and bridle go on (A on the
  saddle rack).
- **Mane braid:** once she is clean, the 5-year-old picks a ribbon colour
  for the braid, with the 4 directions choosing among 4 colours. It shows
  for the rest of the day.
- **Done:** the stall door swings open, the horses trot out and the scene
  wipes to the trail. After 90 s, anything left auto-finishes with a
  friendly stable cat helping.

### Stage 2: the trail ride (about 4 minutes)

Side-scrolling countryside in parallax: meadows, a farm, woods, a stream, a
hill with a windmill and a lake. The mare carries the adult's rider (and
the toddler's rider as a passenger behind the saddle). The foal carries
nobody, but wears the 5-year-old's hat colour.

- **Mare (adult):**
  - Right on the stick speeds up (walk → trot → canter, with the hoof
    rhythm in the sound) and left slows down.
  - A jumps: a log, a stream or a hay bale. A **jump assist** makes the
    jump start at the right moment within a window of ±0.4 s.
  - A missed jump means she just steps over, a bit slower, with a funny
    "hup!".
  - She can never fall or get hurt.
- **Foal (5-year-old):**
  - It runs in one of three lanes (near path, middle, meadow edge) and
    stays within ±40 px of the mare.
  - Up and down change lanes, and A hops to grab something.
  - Collectibles in the lanes: apples (count toward the show), wildflowers
    (the braid at the show), and a rare lucky horseshoe (it goes on the
    stable wall and is kept between sessions).
  - The foal jumps small logs by itself.
- **Neigh (2-year-old):**
  - Any press: both horses whinny with a mane toss, and the nearest animal
    reacts. Rabbits pop out of holes, sheep baa and run in a little circle,
    ducks flap on the lake, a cow moos back, birds scatter.
  - **Gates:** each field ends in a gate that opens on a neigh (after 5 s
    it opens by itself).
  - **Big moment:** a wild horse herd gallops along the hill, and a neigh
    makes them all whinny back as the rainbow comes out.
- **Weather and time:** morning sun, then a short warm rain shower (puddles
  to splash through), then golden afternoon light as the trail reaches the
  show grounds.

### Stage 3: the horse show (about 2 minutes)

A show ring with flags, a small crowd of the riders' friends, and a judge
(a friendly old horse in a hat).

1. **The bell (2-year-old):** a press starts each round, ding!, and the
   crowd claps.
2. **The mare's course (adult):** 6 jumps on an automatic path around the
   ring. The adult only presses A to jump, with the same assist. Each clear
   jump pops confetti, and each refusal is a comedic stop, a snort, and
   going around again. The course always finishes.
3. **The foal's mini course (5-year-old):** 4 tiny jumps. The foal runs
   automatically, and the kid presses A at each one, with a huge window.
   Then 4 "pick the colour" poles: the arrows choose the matching coloured
   cone, which is really just for fun, since every choice gets a cheer.
4. **The rosettes:** everybody wins. The mare, the foal and every rider get
   a rosette, and the colour depends on the day (apples, flowers, clean
   jumps), but all of them are celebrated the same. The judge nods and
   gives the foal a carrot.

### Party: the rosette wall

Back in the stable at dusk:
- the day's rosettes are pinned on the wall next to the rosettes from every
  earlier session (kept between sessions, as in Submarine Safari's album);
- the lucky horseshoes hang over the door;
- the horses eat hay and the foal lies down to sleep.

### No-fail rules

- No falls, injuries, lost horses or timers that end anything. Every step
  that waits for one seat completes itself after 5 s.
- A missed jump is a slower step-over; an unscrubbed patch is finished by
  the stable cat; the foal can never leave the tether.

## Screens and flow

join → select → stable → trail → show → party → select.

- **join:** copied from PixelPals, with new role icons: a horseshoe, a
  brush and a whinnying horse head.
- **select** (the horse fun starts here):
  - The adult picks the **mare's coat** (bay, chestnut, palomino, grey,
    black, pinto) and the 5-year-old picks the **foal's coat** (the same
    list plus appaloosa spots).
  - Each seat picks a rider, from the roster plus the private pack; riders
    wear a little riding helmet in the seat colour.
  - The start stage is picked from 3 icons.
- **pause:** as in PixelPals, controlled by the Rider only.

Horse coats need browns, tans and creams that Sweetie 16 lacks. Use
Submarine Safari's `sprites.py`, which has palette remapping, so the rider
grids from Sweetie 16 still work.

## Audio (our synth)

| Sounds |
|---|
| `neigh_1..4` (formant synth plus a pitch wobble; the foal's is higher), `snort`, `nicker` (soft greeting) |
| hooves: `walk_loop`, `trot_loop`, `canter_loop` (a clip-clop rhythm locked to speed), `jump_hup`, `land_thud` |
| stable: `brush_scrub`, `mud_poof`, `munch`, `hoof_pick_ping`, `horseshoe_clink`, `saddle_creak`, `door_creak`, `stable_cat_meow` |
| trail: `gate_open`, `apple_pop`, `flower_pick`, `lucky_chime`, `splash`, `rain_loop`, `rainbow`, `baa`, `quack`, `moo`, `bunny_boing`, `wild_herd` |
| show: `bell_ding`, `crowd_clap`, `crowd_ooh`, `confetti`, `rosette_fanfare` |
| music: `music_stable` (cosy, 84 BPM), `music_trail` (a jaunty 6/8 "trot" feel at 126 BPM, beats aligned to samples), `music_show` (fanfare-ish), `music_menu` |

Before planning the sound, read Garage Band's and Zoo Keeper's notes: the
formant voices come from Zoo Keeper, and the loop-seam rules from the
skill.

## Reuse

- **shared:** `players.gd`, `art.gd`, `sfx.gd` and `shots.py`.
- **Submarine Safari:** `sprites.py` with palette remapping; `save.gd` for
  the rosette wall and the horseshoes.
- **Space Mission:** the helmet-overlay generator, adapted to a riding
  helmet.
- **Zoo Keeper:** the formant synth, for the neighs.
- **Pillow Fort:** the lifting/hotspot pattern, for the grooming patches.
- **PixelPals:** the join/select/party screens and the demo driver;
  side-scroll parallax.

## New tech

- **Coat recolouring:** one set of horse grids, and a generator swaps the
  base, mane and hoof colours per coat, then adds overlay masks for pinto
  and appaloosa. The lint checks every coat stays in the palette.
- **Mare and foal tether:** the foal's x position is spring-bound to the
  mare's, and its lane changes are eased.
- **Jump assist:** a jump window keyed to the obstacle's distance, and a
  step-over fallback.
- **Speed-locked hoof loops:** the clip-clop tempo follows the gait (pick
  loop lengths that are whole beats).
- **Persistent rosette wall** via `save.gd` (versioned JSON in `user://`,
  with separate demo and test files, as in Submarine Safari).
- **Risks:** horse legs animated at 40x32 must read as walk, trot and
  canter; check them in contact sheets at 1x, 4x and in silhouette.
  Endesga palette remap traps (Space Mission needed `REMAP_OVERRIDES`).
